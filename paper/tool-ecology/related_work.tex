\paragraph{Reusable tools.} LATM separates tool making from cached use~\cite{cai2024latm}; CREATOR separates abstract creation from concrete execution~\cite{qian2023creator}. Voyager accumulates executable embodied skills~\cite{wang2023voyager}, while CRAFT builds and retrieves toolsets~\cite{yuan2024craft}. ToolLibGen uses Code and Reviewing agents~\cite{yue2025toollibgen}; CollabToolBuilder assigns builder/critic roles with human validation~\cite{daull2025collabtoolbuilder}. ToolSmith constructs and tests wrappers~\cite{vakudavathu2026toolsmith}; CoAgent couples concurrent agents to tools through a privileged builder~\cite{lyu2026coagent}; SMITH jointly optimizes creation and use~\cite{tam2026smith}. These precedents establish executable capability growth. Our focus is role-free contributions under repeated local guidance, with adoption, ownership and collective coverage distinguished.

\paragraph{Teams and collective outcomes.} MetaGPT and ChatDev prescribe software-development roles/procedures~\cite{hong2024metagpt,qian2024chatdev}; AgentVerse recruits and revises expert teams~\cite{chen2024agentverse}; AutoGen supports programmable interaction and execution feedback~\cite{wu2024autogen}. Assigned roles do not establish spontaneous differentiation. Generative Agents studies information diffusion and social coordination~\cite{park2023generative}. Pairwise interaction supports conventions, with committed-agent interventions probing stability~\cite{ashery2025conventions}. Riedl distinguishes dynamical coupling from goal-aligned organization using information-theoretic measures and prompt interventions~\cite{riedl2026coordination}. We examine persistent functional contributions rather than infer organization solely from task success.

\paragraph{Local interaction and swarm mechanisms.} Nisioti et al. study innovation under different topologies in Little Alchemy 2~\cite{nisioti2024collective}; cognitive-agent networks study exploration and convergence~\cite{zomer2026collective}. Adaptive transmission programs make information routing intervenable~\cite{colas2026transmission}. GPTSwarm optimizes agent computational graphs~\cite{zhuge2024gptswarm}, while particle-swarm methods use language models for population search~\cite{shinohara2025lmpso}. Such swarm terminology does not identify Reynolds's mechanism. Existing LLM-driven flocking and swarm simulations already use physical motion~\cite{li2024flocking,jimenezromero2025swarm,rahman2025llmswarms}; we make no first-Boids-with-LLMs claim.

\paragraph{Persistent ecologies.} TerraLingua studies resource-constrained ecologies with persistent artifacts~\cite{paolo2026terralingua}. Conversable Complexity proposes interpretable agent collectives with memory, tools and shared skills~\cite{najarro2026conversable}. These precede broad artifact-mediated emergence claims. Our specific contribution is a completed executable-tool study separating correct adoption, task-relative functional evidence, author capability and complementary coverage. This focused review supports that distinction, not an exhaustive census or universal priority claim.
