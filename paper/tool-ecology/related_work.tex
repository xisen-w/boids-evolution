\subsection{Creating tools and maintaining reusable libraries}
Tool creation provides a precedent for persistent executable capabilities. LATM separates making tools from using cached functions~\cite{cai2024latm}. CREATOR distinguishes tool creation from reasoning with generated tools~\cite{qian2023creator}. Voyager accumulates executable skills in an open-ended embodied environment~\cite{wang2023voyager}, while CRAFT explicitly constructs and retrieves reusable toolsets~\cite{yuan2024craft}. These works motivate reusable capabilities without establishing that interactions among multiple autonomous builders are necessary.

Recent systems organize tool construction directly. ToolLibGen uses Code and Reviewing agents to assemble reusable libraries~\cite{yue2025toollibgen}; CollabToolBuilder assigns complementary roles~\cite{daull2025collabtoolbuilder}. ToolSmith demonstrates multi-agent construction and testing of wrappers~\cite{vakudavathu2026toolsmith}. CoAgent couples concurrent agents to a library through a privileged builder~\cite{lyu2026coagent}, and SMITH studies a policy for creation and use~\cite{tam2026smith}. We therefore claim neither the first multi-agent tool builder nor the first growing executable library. Our object is the organization of role-free contributions under repeated local guidance, with adoption, ownership and collective coverage measured separately.

\subsection{Prescribed teams and collective behavior as an outcome}
MetaGPT and ChatDev organize software development through assigned roles and procedures~\cite{hong2024metagpt,qian2024chatdev}. AgentVerse recruits and revises expert roles~\cite{chen2024agentverse}; AutoGen supports programmable multi-agent interaction patterns and execution feedback~\cite{wu2024autogen}. Their structured coordination is relevant engineering context. Assigned roles, however, do not establish spontaneous differentiation.

Other work treats group behavior itself as the outcome. Generative Agents studies information diffusion and social coordination~\cite{park2023generative}. Repeated pairwise interaction can support convention formation, with committed-agent interventions probing stability~\cite{ashery2025conventions}. Riedl separates dynamical coupling from goal-aligned organization through prompt interventions and shuffled references~\cite{riedl2026coordination}. These precedents motivate distinguishing collective structure from overall success. Executable tools add persistent functional contributions whose effects can be inspected on fixed computations.

\subsection{Local interaction, innovation and swarm analogies}
Collective Innovation in Groups of Large Language Models studies topology and adoption in Little Alchemy~\cite{nisioti2024collective}. Its copying mechanisms illustrate why exposure and faithful adoption should be distinguished. Cognitive-agent networks also provide a setting for studying exploration and convergence~\cite{zomer2026collective}. Adaptive transmission programs make information routing itself an intervenable object~\cite{colas2026transmission}. These are explanatory precedents rather than merely accuracy baselines.

GPTSwarm optimizes language-agent computational graphs~\cite{zhuge2024gptswarm}. Particle-swarm approaches translate population search into proposals from language models~\cite{shinohara2025lmpso}. Their swarm terminology does not imply Reynolds's separation, alignment and cohesion mechanism. Our triad is a bundled guidance policy, and its effects cannot be identified as the original physical dynamics.

\subsection{Boids and interpretable persistent ecologies}
Independent work already studies LLM-driven flocking and swarm simulations~\cite{li2024flocking,jimenezromero2025swarm,rahman2025llmswarms}. We do not claim the first use of Boids with language models. Our domain concerns cognitive contributions through tools rather than physical positions and headings.

TerraLingua studies local perception with persistent editable text artifacts~\cite{paolo2026terralingua}. Conversable Complexity explicitly proposes interpretable agent collectives with memory, connectivity and self-extensible tools and skills~\cite{najarro2026conversable}. They directly precede broad claims about persistent artifacts as a substrate for emergence. Our specific contribution is the completed executable-tool study and its separation of correct adoption, task-relative functional evidence, author capability and complementary coverage. The literature review is focused and inherited from the preceding draft, not an exhaustive census or a universal priority claim.
